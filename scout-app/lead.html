⚡LEAD TOOLS⚡

Make the QR codes that set up scout iPads, until the scan-station laptop app does this (Milestone 2). Codes are made in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

1 · iPad config codes

Each iPad scans the code for its station once. The roster lets scouts pick their own name at shift change.

2 · Schedule codes

One line per match: match, red1, red2, red3, blue1, blue2, blue3. Match keys: qm12, playoffs sf3m1, finals f1m2. Hold the codes up to each iPad's camera (Home → Scan schedule); parts can be scanned in any order.

3 · Check a scout's QR code

Scan a record from an iPad with this computer's webcam (or paste its text) to confirm it decodes.
