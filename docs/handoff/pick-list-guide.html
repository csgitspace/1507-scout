The pick list collects everything our scouts record and turns it into a ranked list for alliance selection.

  • You can filter and tag teams, add notes, compare teams on charts, and track who's been picked as it happens.
  • It updates by itself about every 3 minutes during the event.
  • It never changes what the scouts recorded.

Screenshots in this guide use made-up sample data.

1. Getting in

  1. Open the link above on your phone and sign in with your @warlocks1507.com Google account. Other accounts can't open it.
  2. The first time, Google asks you to authorize the app. It may say "unverified", which is normal for the team's own app. Tick every box on that screen, then Continue. If you leave one unticked, the app asks again.
  3. Add it to your home screen so it's one tap away at 4pm Saturday.

Opens instantly

After your first visit, the phone shows the last pick list you loaded right away, with saved copy, updating… in the header. A moment later the fresh one replaces it. While the page is open, it refreshes every minute.

Is it up to date?

The green chip in the header says Synced N min ago. That's the last time the scan station pushed new scouting data. It turns red with "STALE" after 10 minutes. If you see that, tell the scouting lead (usually the phone tether at the scan station).

The four screens

  • Pick list: the ranked list (most of this guide).
  • Next match: the six teams in our next match, with their stats and notes.
  • Data health: missing scans, scouting mistakes, and how fresh the data is.
  • Analyze: charts and team comparison (section 5).

2. Official list and My list

There are two lists. Switch between them at the top of the Pick list screen.

  • Official list: the team's pick order, tags and shared notes.
    • Only pick leads can change it. Pick leads are the people the team account lists on the Leads tab of the scouting Sheet.
    • Everyone else sees it read-only. The line under the switch says Viewer.
  • My list: your own practice list. Rank and tag teams however you like; only you see it, and it never changes the official list.
    • Start from the official list copies the official order and tags as a starting point.
    • Clear my list empties it.
    • Cards show an Official: #3 Target badge so you can compare.

If nobody is on the Leads tab yet, everyone can edit the official list, and the page shows a warning.

3. Reading a team card

  • Rank (gold number) and the ⠿ handle.
    • Drag the handle, or tap ▲▼, to reorder. It saves for everyone on the official list, or just for you on My list.
    • Reordering is off while a filter or sort is on.
  • Badges:
    • Scoring High/Mid/Low
    • bot type (Scorer, Feeder, Defense or Hybrid)
    • Strength (draft)
    • Fit, with the reason, e.g. "Fit High: L3 80%, avg 41 fuel"
    Section 6 explains how each is worked out.
  • Scouted stats: fuel per match (auto in brackets), shooting accuracy, best climb level and how often, matches scouted. Any reliability problems appear after ⚠ (no-show, broke down, dead in auto).
  • Outside data (blue line): Statbotics EPA, The Blue Alliance OPR, event rank and record. "Season EPA" means Statbotics doesn't track this event, so the number is from the team's whole 2026 season.
  • Target / Backup / Avoid tags. The card's left edge turns green, blue or red.
  • Shared note: visible to the whole pick team, signed with who wrote it. Only pick leads can edit it.
  • My private note: only you see it in the app. It saves when you tap outside the box.
  • Scout notes (N): tap to read what scouts wrote about this robot, newest first.

Our robot needs

Pick leads tap the gaps in our robot (More scoring, Auto scoring, Reliable climber, Level 3 climber, Defender, Feeder). Every team's Fit is worked out against those gaps, so set them before you sort by Fit.

Filters and sort

Narrow the list by Scoring, Bot type, Strength, Fit or Tag, or hide picked teams. Sort by Pick order, EPA, Scouted fuel or TBA rank. Team 1507 isn't on the list, since we can't pick ourselves.

4. During alliance selection

  1. One pick lead keeps the official list open. Each time another alliance takes a team, they tap Picked on its card.
  2. Everyone sees that card dimmed, with the number crossed out and a PICKED badge. The pick order does not change.
  3. A gold Best available box appears at the top. It shows the top three teams still left, in list order, and skips anything tagged Avoid. That's the answer when it's our turn. On My list, it follows your own order.
  4. Set the Picked filter to Hide picked to see only teams still available.

After the event, a pick lead taps Clear all picked (in the Best available box) to reset.

Tip

Have the pick leads finish the official order before 4pm. During selection, only tap Picked and avoid reordering. Everyone's Best available stays correct as long as the list itself isn't changing.

5. Analyze: charts and comparison

Tap Analyze in the header.

  • The charts use the same data as the pick list. The first time you open the screen it needs a connection to load the chart tool.
  • Tap any dot or bar to see the team and its numbers.
  • Team 1507 is left out of the charts but included in the alliance builder.

Compare teams

Choose up to three teams. The table lines up their scouted stats, EPA, OPR and TBA rank, and the best value in each row is gold. It starts with the top two of the official list.

Match by match

Fuel each compared team scored in each of its matches, in the order they played. A gap means a no-show. This shows whether a team is improving, fading, or had one bad match that pulls its average down.

Alliance builder

Us plus the first two compared teams:

  • Total fuel per match, compared with a typical alliance here (three median teams). Green means we'd beat it.
  • Fuel in auto.
  • How many robots we'd expect to climb, and how many to reach Level 3.
  • Combined EPA.
  • All working: the three teams' reliability scores multiplied together. It's a rough guide to how often all three robots would get through a match.

Scoring vs reliability

One dot per team: fuel per match across, reliability up. Dashed lines mark the event medians.

  • Top-right teams score a lot and keep working. Start your list here.
  • Bottom-right teams score well when they work but break down or don't show. Fine as a second pick only if you can live with the risk.
  • Dot colors are the official tags; ✕ marks picked teams.

Consistency

Each bar runs from a team's worst match to its best, and the gold mark is its average.

  • A short bar means you know what you'll get.
  • A long bar means the average hides big swings. Check its scout notes.

Our scouting vs EPA

Each dot compares a team's Statbotics EPA (its expected point contribution, from the whole season) with our scouted fuel per match. They should rise together along the dashed trend line.

  • The r value under the chart says how well they agree: 1 is perfect, above 0.7 is strong.
  • The three teams furthest from the line are named. Above the line means they're doing better for us than their season suggests (improved robot, or a scouting error). Below means worse (a struggling robot, or a scout who undercounted).
  • Either way, it's worth a second look before you rank them.

Auto vs teleop, and Climbing

Stacked bars for the top 16 teams:

  • Average fuel split into auto and teleop.
  • How often each team finished at Level 3, Level 2, Level 1 or no climb.

6. Where the numbers come from

Scoutwatches one robot, records on an offline iPad
QR codecarries the record to the scan station
Scan stationchecks and stores every record
Syncevery ~3 min over a phone's cellular data
Google Sheetrebuilds team stats, which the pick list reads

Every sync, the Sheet keeps every record it has ever received, then rebuilds each team's numbers from scratch:

  1. Corrections replace the original. If a scout fixes a record, only the newest version counts. The old one is kept but not used.
  2. One robot, one match, one observation. If two scouts watched the same robot in the same match, their numbers are averaged so that match doesn't count twice.
  3. No-shows don't drag down ability. Scoring, accuracy and climbing averages skip matches where the robot never came on the field. No-shows still count against reliability.
  4. Averages per match for fuel (auto, teleop, total) and climb rates. Accuracy = fuel scored ÷ (scored + missed), auto misses included.
Shown asHow it's worked out
Scoring High / Mid / LowTeams ranked by average fuel per match and split into thirds for this event.
Climb (e.g. L3 40%)Share of played matches that ended at that level or higher.
Reliability100% minus the no-show rate, minus the broke-down rate, minus half the dead-in-auto rate.
Bot typeThe role scouts recorded most often (Scorer / Feeder / Defense). Hybrid if no role reaches 55% of matches.
Strength (draft)Scoring rank × reliability, split into thirds.
Fit (draft)How well the team covers the gaps ticked under "Our robot needs", scaled down for low reliability. High at 60% or more, Mid at 30% or more.
EPA, OPR, TBA rankFetched from Statbotics and The Blue Alliance. Shown alongside our numbers, never mixed into them.

Keep in mind

  • Small samples. At a one-day event each team plays about 8–10 matches, so one great or terrible match moves an average a lot. Check Consistency and the scout notes, and use EPA as a second opinion.
  • Strength and Fit are drafts. The scouting leads haven't settled what they should mean. Use them to sort, not to decide.
  • Privacy. Private notes and My lists are hidden from others in the app. Anyone who opens the scouting Sheet itself can still see those tabs.
